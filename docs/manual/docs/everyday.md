# Everyday tasks

Short answers to the things people actually do every day. Each one links to
the full page if you need more.

If you are new: you do not need to read this manual. Read this page.

---

## Getting around

The **sidebar** on the left is every part of the system. You only see the
parts your role allows — if a colleague has a menu you do not, that is
deliberate, not a fault.

**Press `Ctrl` + `K`** anywhere to search everything at once: a customer, an
invoice number, a product, a supplier. It is the fastest way to find
anything.

Your name at the **bottom of the sidebar** is your own account — change your
password there. The **ع / EN** button at the top switches language.

## Selling

### Quote a customer

**Quotations → + Add quotation.** Pick the customer, add your lines, save.

Start typing in the item box and it searches your inventory — pick an item
and the price fills in for you. You can always type over it.

Not sure what a box is for? **Hover over it** and a short description
appears.

### Turn a quote into an invoice

Open the quotation and choose **Convert to Invoice**. Everything carries
across; you do not retype it.

### Bill a customer

**Invoices → + Add invoice.** Same idea as a quotation.

### Record a payment

Open the invoice and click **Record Payment**. Enter the amount — it can be
part of the total, and you can record several payments over time. The status
(Unpaid → Partial → Paid) works itself out.

### Send it to the customer

Click **Send** on the row and choose WhatsApp or email. See
[Sending invoices & quotations](sales/sending.md).

### See everything for one customer

**Clients → the customer.** Their quotations, invoices and projects are all
on that page, and you can click an invoice number to open it.

## Finding things

The lists show **one page at a time**, newest first.

Type in the **search box** above a list to search *everything*, not just the
page you are looking at. Click a **column heading** to sort — again, the
whole list, not the visible page.

So if you cannot see an old invoice, do not scroll: search for it.

**Export** gives you everything that matches your search, not just the page
on screen.

## Stock

### Add a product

**Inventory → + Add Item.** Only the **name** is required. In practice you
want three things: the name, what it **costs** you, and what you **sell** it
for.

Worth setting while you are there:

- **Min Stock Alert** — you get told when it runs low, instead of finding out
  from a customer.
- **Category** — you can type a new one right in the box; nothing to set up
  first.
- **Initial Quantity** — what is on the shelf today. Only offered when you
  create the item; after that stock moves through purchases, sales and
  adjustments, never by typing over the number.

Cost and price each have a currency box beside them. Full detail in
[Inventory](operations/inventory.md#adding-an-item).

### Scan a barcode

You can scan into the **Barcode** box when adding a product, and the form
will not save early when the scanner beeps.

Elsewhere, scanning just works: at the till it adds the item to the sale,
and in the inventory search it finds the item.

The system stores and reads barcodes that already exist on your products. It
does **not** print barcode labels.

### Check stock

**Inventory** shows what you have. Items below their minimum are
highlighted, and you get a notification.

## Money

### Take a payment at the till

**Point of Sale.** Scan or tap items, take the money, done — it creates the
invoice and the payment for you.

### Record an expense

**Expenses → + Add expense.**

### End of day

**Cash → Reconciliation.** Count the drawer, enter what you counted, and the
system shows you any difference.

## When something looks wrong

**Something is missing from my menu.** Your role does not include it. Ask
your administrator.

**A number looks wrong.** Nothing is deleted in this system. Open the record
and check its history, or ask an administrator to look at the audit trail.

**I made a mistake on an invoice.** If no payment has been recorded, edit
it. If a payment has, **void** it instead — voiding keeps the record and the
reason, which is what an accountant needs.

**A message says my licence is expiring.** Tell whoever handles your
account. Nothing is lost; see [Your account](foundation/your-account.md).

**Something is broken.** Use **Report a problem** in the top bar. It sends
the page, the error and what you were doing — you do not have to explain the
technical part.

## Two habits worth having

**Search instead of scrolling.** `Ctrl` + `K`, or the search box above any
list.

**Hover when unsure.** Fields that are not obvious explain themselves.
