Questions

Frequently asked

Is Cedaris really self-hosted?

Yes. Cedaris runs on your own Windows PC, office server, or private cloud. No mandatory vendor cloud — your database stays with you permanently.

Who owns my data?

You do — completely. The data lives in a database on your machine. Back it up, move it, or export it any time. No vendor can lock you out.

Does it support USD and LBP simultaneously?

Dual currency is built into the core. Set your own exchange rate (with full history) and quote, sell, reconcile and report in both currencies side by side.

Is Arabic fully supported with RTL layout?

Every screen, document and report is available in English and Arabic with proper right-to-left layout. Switch languages in one click with no context loss.

Do I need a constant internet connection?

No. Cedaris runs on your local network — ideal for shops, warehouses and restaurant counters where internet reliability can't be guaranteed.

Can I start with a few modules and add more later?

Absolutely. All modules share the same database — activate more as you grow with no painful migrations between them.

How is it priced?

A license you own, sized to the modules you use — not a per-seat monthly rental. Request a demo and we'll put together a transparent quote.
